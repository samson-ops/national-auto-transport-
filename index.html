Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to ship a car?
Most coast-to-coast shipments run $1,300–$1,700 for a standard sedan on an open carrier; shorter routes (under 500 miles) often run $450–$700. Price depends on distance, vehicle size, running condition, season, and open vs. enclosed transport. Get an exact quote above — it's free and takes about a minute.
How long does auto transport take?
Carriers cover roughly 400–500 miles per day. Regional moves usually deliver in 1–3 days, mid-range routes in 3–6 days, and coast-to-coast in 7–10 days. Pickup windows are typically 1–5 business days from your first available date.
Is my car insured during shipping?
Yes. Federal law requires every carrier to hold active cargo insurance, and we verify the certificate before dispatch. Your vehicle is covered from the moment it's loaded until it's delivered. We'll gladly send you the carrier's insurance certificate on request.
Should I choose open or enclosed transport?
Open transport is what 90%+ of customers use — it's the same method dealerships use for new cars and costs significantly less. Enclosed transport is worth it for classics, exotics, and vehicles valued over about $70,000, since it protects against weather and road debris.
Do I pay anything upfront?
No. Getting a quote and booking costs nothing. You pay a portion when a carrier is dispatched (usually by card) and the balance to the driver at delivery by cash, cashier's check, or Zelle. If we never dispatch a carrier, you owe nothing.
Can I put personal items in the car?
Most carriers allow up to 100 lbs of personal items in the trunk or below window level. Items aren't covered by cargo insurance, so keep valuables with you. Let us know at booking so we note it on the dispatch.
Can you ship a car that doesn't run?
Yes — we ship inoperable vehicles regularly. Just tell us at booking so we assign a carrier with a winch. Non-running vehicles typically add $100–$200 to the quote.
