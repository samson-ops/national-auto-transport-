Portland Car Shipping FAQ

How fast can you pick up a vehicle in Portland?
Standard pickup windows in the Portland area run 1–5 business days from your first available date; on busy corridors it's often 1–3. Need it faster? Expedited dispatch targets pickup within 24–48 hours.
How much does it cost to ship a car from Portland?
See the route table above for recent averages from Portland. Your exact price depends on vehicle size, running condition, transport type, and season — quotes are free, take about a minute, and are all-in with no hidden fees.
Do you pick up outside Portland itself?
Yes — we cover the entire metro area including Beaverton, Gresham, Hillsboro, Lake Oswego, Tigard and beyond. Door-to-door service is standard everywhere the carrier can safely and legally operate.
Do I pay anything upfront?
No. You pay nothing until a carrier is assigned and your pickup is scheduled. Get a quote, book, and cancel free anytime before dispatch.
